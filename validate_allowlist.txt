# An allowlist entry is a claim that the data is as good as it can get. It ages

VN: Wikidata files Vietnam's subdivision classes -- commune-level town (535 items), district-level town (114), provincial city (91), urban district (54), rural district (47) -- beneath "former subdivisions of Vietnam", which is beneath "former administrative territorial entity". Excluding what upstream itself marks as former is correct, so the shortfall is upstream state rather than a classification bug. Recheck after a dump refresh: if the successor units are populated under new classes the count recovers on its own. Partly recovered already (102 -> 138) now that settlements reaching no division are kept.

PA: 197 settlements exist in Wikidata by P17 and coordinate, of which 183 attach to one of the 14 selected provinces -- a 93% attachment rate, so containment is working. The gap against a 551-city baseline is that Wikidata carries roughly a third of what the ODbL dataset does for Panama.

UY: 852 settlements exist, 846 attach (99%). Confirmed independently against direct SPARQL, which found ~816. The 2,010-city baseline is far ahead of what Wikidata records for Uruguay, and the language fallback recovered nothing further, so this is not a labelling problem either.

CR: 108 settlements exist, 99 attach (92%). The 159-city baseline exceeds what Wikidata holds for Costa Rica.
